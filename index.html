How to connect Google Sheets

1. Create a new Google Sheet.
2. Go to Extensions → Apps Script.
3. Paste the script provided separately, and deploy it as a Web App (access: "Anyone").
4. Copy the deployment URL and paste it above.
5. Once connected, every shift signup and payment entry syncs to your Sheet automatically.

Data is saved on this device and, once connected, synced to your Google Sheet. No patient data leaves your organization's sheet.
